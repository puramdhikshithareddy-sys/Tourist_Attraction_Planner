Algorithms Behind the Planner

Greedy

Chooses the best locally feasible option at each step.

Input›Score›Sort›Select›Final Plan
O(n log n)

0/1 Knapsack

Finds a high-value combination under resource constraints. It evaluates combinations systematically instead of choosing only the best immediate option.

Items›Dynamic Programming›Optimal Combination
O(n × T × B)

Graph Traversal

Explores relationships between attractions.

Nodes›Edges›BFS/DFS›Traversal
O(V + E)
